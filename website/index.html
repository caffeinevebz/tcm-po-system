Welcome to the ministry

A house of worship for the humble coffee bean.

The Caffeine Ministry is a neighbourhood café for long conversations and short espressos. Every cup is weighed, timed and poured by baristas who care far more than is strictly necessary.

Pull up a chair by the window, flick through a book from the shelf, and let the afternoon go slowly. That's the whole idea.

The three commandments

What we believe in

i.

Good beans, honestly brewed

Fresh-roasted coffee from Indian estates, dialled in every morning and brewed exactly to recipe.

ii.

Food worth lingering over

All-day plates and bakes made in our kitchen, from a proper breakfast to a late-afternoon slice.

iii.

A room to slow down in

Old radios, warm lamps and art on the walls. Bring a friend, a laptop or nobody at all.
