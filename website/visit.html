Questions

Do I need to book?

No. Walk-ins are always welcome. Booking simply guarantees you a table, which helps on weekend evenings.

How do I book for a big group?

For up to 12 guests, use the booking page. For larger groups or to hire the space, send an events enquiry.

How do I see the menu?

The full menu is online, and each table has a QR code that opens it on your phone.

What if I'm running late or need to cancel?

Just let us know as soon as you can, using the phone number or WhatsApp above, so we can give the table to someone else.

Save yourself a seat.

Walk-ins are always welcome. Booking just means your table is waiting.
